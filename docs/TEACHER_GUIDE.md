# Teacher Guide

## What students do

Students manage a small Mars outpost for 30 sols. They plan jobs, watch meters,
and choose safe actions during emergencies. Every choice has a trade-off.

## Classroom use

Begin with Training. Ask students to predict what happens before each choice.
Then use a Classroom Mission in pairs. Ask: “What did your team give up?”
No account or personal data is needed. The game saves only on the device.

## Discussion

Compare plans that protect power, air, shield, and wellbeing. Ask why Earth
messages take time. Invite students to explain one real, simplified, and game
value from the Mission Report.
